THE VAULT

Nine kinds of secret.

Logins

Passwords, sites, custom fields, and a built-in 2FA code per login.

2FA codes

A full authenticator. QR setup by camera, Steam supported, works offline.

Secure notes

Free-form encrypted text.

Cards

Number, expiry, CVV and PIN. The last two ask for your fingerprint.

Identities

Passport, licence, national ID, address. Type it in or photograph the document.

Files & media

Documents, photos and video, encrypted. Video plays inside the app, never in the gallery.

Passkeys

WebAuthn credentials, created and used on this device.

GPS locations

One reading, taken when you tap. The app does not follow you afterwards.

Recovery phrases

BIP-39 words in numbered order, checksum-checked as you type. Optional 25th word.

folders · tags · favourites · drag to move, one tap to undo · trash restores whole folders

DAY TO DAY

Everyday use, fully offline.

Autofill

Fills apps and browsers, saves new logins, updates changed ones. A password saved in Chrome fills in Firefox.

Capture to vault

Photograph documents straight into the vault. Nothing touches your gallery; camera and location data are stripped.

Generator

Passwords and passphrases, with a strength meter.

Search

Titles, usernames, sites, notes and folders. Typing your secret phrase here reveals invisible entries.

Import

Google, Apple, Bitwarden, 1Password, KeePass, Aegis and CSV, each with step-by-step instructions. Imports merge and are never capped.

Health scan

Finds reused, weak, old, duplicate and breached passwords. The breach list ships inside the app.

BY DESIGN

Never.

No vendor can reset your master password. Lose it and the Recovery Kit, and the vault is gone — the app says exactly that at setup.

One thing does leave the phone, if you let it: Android's own backup can copy the encrypted vault file to your Google account, the way it does for any app. We never see it, and it is useless without your master password — but it is still a copy somewhere else, so the app puts the switch in Backup & export and tells you which way it is set.

  • ✕No sync server — nothing to breach centrally
  • ✕No account, no email, no telemetry, no crash reporting
  • ✕No vendor password reset — there is no vendor-side anything
  • ✕No browser extension, desktop app or web vault
  • ✕No light theme — dark only, by decision
