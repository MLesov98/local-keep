LocalKeep keeps your passwords in an encrypted vault on your phone.

There is no account to create and no sync server to trust. Your master password protects your vault, and nobody can reset it for you — not even us. A Recovery Kit can restore access if you forget it. Keep the Kit offline: anyone who has it can open your real vault.

WHAT IT DOES

• Logins and secure notes, with no limit on how many
• A password and passphrase generator you control — length, character classes, word count
• Autofill in apps and browsers, plus passkey support on Android 14 and later
• Two-factor codes stored beside the login they belong to, with a QR scanner
• Biometric unlock, and an auto-lock timeout you choose
• A password health check for reused, weak, old and potentially breached passwords, using a bundled offline list so no passwords or derived data are sent for checking
• Import from KeePass (KDBX), Bitwarden, 1Password, Chrome, Apple Passwords, Aegis, or any CSV with a header row
• Export whenever you like — it is your file
• Local snapshots and a recoverable trash, so a mistake is not permanent
• A Pro duress vault: a second master password opens a decoy vault. Biometric unlock and the Recovery Kit still open your real vault

FREE AND PRO

Logins and notes are unlimited. The generator, autofill, two-factor codes, attachments, import, export and the health check are free, with no ads.

Pro is a one-time purchase — no subscription — that adds the duress vault and removes the free-tier limits. Free includes one card, one identity entry, and up to seven files, photos or attachments in total.

WHAT THE APP COLLECTS

LocalKeep collects no analytics, crash reports or telemetry. This version connects to Google Play only for Pro purchases, entitlement checks and an occasional in-app review card. No vault data is sent to Google Play. Android itself may still report a crash to Google if you have system diagnostics turned on — that is the operating system, not LocalKeep, and such a report never contains vault contents, passwords or keys.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROTECT YOU FROM

Your vault is encrypted with a key derived from your master password, so a weak master password is the weak link, and no password manager can fix that for you. If the phone itself is compromised at the operating-system level — rooted, or running a hostile build of Android — then anything you type can be read before it reaches the app. That is true of every password manager, and we would rather say it than let you assume otherwise.

LocalKeep is open source. Read the code before you trust it with anything.
